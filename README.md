# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes agricultural performance across different seasons using data related to crops, environmental conditions, irrigation methods, resource usage, production, and economic outcomes.

The main purpose of the project is to identify meaningful seasonal patterns, compare agricultural performance, examine resource usage, and generate data-driven insights for agricultural planning.

---

## Problem Statement

Agricultural activities are influenced by seasonal variations in environmental conditions, farming practices, resource availability, and market conditions. As a result, agricultural performance may differ from one season to another.

This project analyzes the available agricultural dataset to investigate seasonal differences in agricultural performance and identify meaningful patterns, trends, relationships, and variations.

---

## Objectives

- Explore and understand the agricultural dataset.
- Clean and prepare the data for analysis.
- Compare agricultural performance across seasons.
- Analyze crop-wise performance.
- Examine crop and season combinations.
- Analyze irrigation methods and resource usage.
- Study environmental conditions across seasons.
- Identify relationships between agricultural variables.
- Visualize important patterns and trends.
- Develop data-driven findings and recommendations.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains agricultural records covering:

- States and districts
- Different crops
- Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons
- Farm area
- Rainfall
- Temperature
- Humidity
- Soil conditions
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Irrigation methods
- Yield and production
- Market price
- Cost and revenue
- Profit
- Water usage and efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

The dataset contains approximately **4,000 records and 28 variables**.

## Key Findings

1. Kharif shows the strongest overall agricultural and economic performance.
2. Rabi shows moderate performance compared with Kharif and Zaid.
3. Zaid shows the weakest average economic performance.
4. Kharif shows strong water-use performance.
5. Disease and pest risk is highest during Kharif.
6. Sugarcane and Chilli show strong average profitability.
7. Wheat, Rice, and Maize show comparatively weaker profitability in certain conditions.
8. Crop performance varies across seasons.
9. Drip irrigation shows strong average yield and profit performance.
10. Environmental conditions differ considerably across seasons.
11. Correlation analysis identifies relationships between agricultural, resource, production, and economic variables.
12. Overall agricultural performance depends on multiple interacting factors.

---

## Recommendations

- Consider seasonal conditions when planning agricultural activities.
- Evaluate crop-season combinations before selecting crops.
- Investigate low-performing crop-season combinations.
- Consider suitable irrigation methods according to crop and seasonal requirements.
- Monitor rainfall, temperature, humidity, and soil moisture.
- Improve water-resource planning.
- Use data-driven approaches for agricultural decision-making.
- Collect additional historical and regional data for future analysis.
